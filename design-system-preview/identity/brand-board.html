Signature: the holographic blur

Sky, aqua, rose and peach fields, blurred together over a cream ground. The blur means "not real yet": it marks estimates, simulations and brand moments. Real balances and transactions stay sharp, in ink.

Use it for

  • Simulations and scenarios: anything estimated.
  • Brand moments: header, empty states, onboarding, the half-disc of the symbol.

Never

  • Behind a real balance, a transaction or a chart of real data.
  • As a glow around an element, or under text other than ink.

Neutral and ink first, the holographic fields as the signature, and one functional blue for today, focus, selection and links. States always carry a sign or a word as well as a colour.

Voice, five rules

  • Say what is real and what is planned. Never present an estimate as a fact. Do: "Planned: rent, 5 Dec." Not: "You will spend €780."
  • Number first, words after. Do: "€1,050.00 left under the ceiling." Not: "You still have some room on this account."
  • Plain verbs, no hype. Do: "Add a planned item." Not: "Take your finances to the next level."
  • Neutral about money. No praise, no guilt. Do: "Lowest balance: €210.00 on 3 Feb." Not: "Uh-oh, careful!"
  • Errors say what happened and what to do. Do: "€50.00 does not fit under the ceiling of Savings A. Choose an account for it." Not: "Invalid transfer."

In use

Synthetic data. Real values are sharp: a solid line, ink on white. Planned values are dashed, after today; points appear only on the month being read. Estimates live on the holographic blur. Today is the blue rule. Point at the chart, or focus it and use the arrow keys, to read a month.
