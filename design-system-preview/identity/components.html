Demain components

The parts every screen of the app is built from, drawn with the identity tokens. Each control keeps the behaviour the app already has (labels, linked errors, native dialog, toasts that name their kind); only shape and colour are new. Amounts are synthetic.

Rules

Regular for text and amounts, Medium for labels, headings and buttons, Bold only where a single figure must dominate. No other family.

The wordmark is set in the same face, Host Grotesk Medium, converted to outlines.

Buttons and their states

Ink primary, one per screen (06:44, 08:16, 12:21); neutral secondary next to it (12:21); text link for a third action (13:19). Destructive red only on the confirm button inside a dialog. Hover and pressed take one tone step, faded over --motion-duration-state; the button never changes size or position, and with reduced motion the step is instant. Disabled uses explicit colours instead of transparency, so its contrast is measured. Pending keeps the fill, changes the label and sets aria-busy.

Adding a transaction

The add-transaction form of the app becomes three steps, retained by the owner on 11 Nov 2026 from the proposals of form-proposals.html. Step 1 takes the type and the amount, typed or tapped on the keypad, under the exact-syntax rule. Step 2 is the label, with suggestions from history, and the category, "Later" sending it to the to-categorize queue. Step 3 recaps and edits in place, picks the date with the date picker, asks for the receiving account of a transfer, and keeps the note and the payment mode under "More details". Every choice is a chip or a segment with an icon, a colour and a word. The colour pairs of the flow are measured on form-proposals.html. On a computer, from 760 px of available width, the same flow fits one screen in two columns: the amount, the label and the category on the left, the date, the account and the save button on the right. It is typed on the keyboard, so the keypad, the steps and the recap go; the save button checks the amount and the label. Use the switch to see both layouts. A transfer that does not fit under the ceiling of the receiving account opens a step of its own before saving: what fits, what does not, and where the surplus goes (try Move, 200 to Savings A; 50.00 is left under each synthetic ceiling).

Field states

Hover darkens the border to ink. Focus adds the blue ring, the only place besides today where blue appears. Fields with an exact syntax

When a field expects an exact syntax, the browser refuses any other character as it is typed, pasted or dropped, and says why in a polite live message instead of failing silently. An incomplete value is flagged when the field is left. The server checks the same rule again and stays the authority, since the browser can always be bypassed. An amount takes digits and one comma or point, with the decimals of its currency; spaces in a pasted amount are removed. A currency code takes three letters and is uppercased. A balance that can be negative (an opening balance, a balance check, an imported row) keeps the same amount syntax, without a sign: a Credit or Debit choice next to it gives the side, so the sign never has to be typed.

Feedback

A toast names its kind in words ("Success.", "Error."), the tinted disc only repeats it. A success leaves after 8 seconds unless pointed at or focused; an error stays. Layers fade in and rise (--motion-duration-enter, --motion-offset) and leave faster (--motion-duration-exit); the button below shows a toast arriving and leaving. Loading

A skeleton stands only for data that is really loading, in the place where it will appear: no figure, no label, no amount. What does not depend on the data (a title, a section heading, a caption, a column heading) is shown as it is, never as a block; a heading or caption that holds data (an account name) is a block. Blocks are surface-sunken on a card and surface on the page ground, with the radius of what they stand for: rounded-control for a line, rounded-pill for a button, rounded-card for a tile. A soft band sweeps over each block from left to right, once every --motion-duration-shimmer (1.5 s, proposed), only while the content is loading, and is gone when the content replaces the skeleton. It is the one motion that repeats. With reduced motion the blocks stay still. The region carries aria-busy="true" and one visually hidden status message in words; the blocks are aria-hidden. The content then replaces the skeleton with the motion-enter fade.

Planned items

The planned item screens of DESIGN.md section 17, built from the components above: the Select and date picker field, the type segments and choice chips of the add-transaction flow, the checkbox, the disclosure, the table, the notice and the dialog. Nothing is new but the phone list row, because five columns do not fit 343 px. Real values only, so no blur and no dashed line; the only estimate is the rolling average the user chooses, named "Starting estimate". Amounts are typed without a sign and the type gives it. Try the selects, the date fields, the mode and the buttons that open a dialog. Amounts and names are synthetic. In this reference the page title of each screen is an h3, since the page already has its h1; in the app it is the h1.

Calendar: month grid

Seven columns and a weekday row. From 720 px of page column a day is a 96 x 104 px cell with its balance, its counts in words, "Below zero" and "Unplaced surplus"; under it the cell is 49 x 56 px with a solid dot (real entries), a hollow ring (planned entries) and a bar (below zero), explained by the legend line, and everything else is in the details panel. Today (21) has the blue ring, the blue number and the word "Today"; 28 is selected (ink fill), so both states show: select 21 to see a selected day that is also today (drawn selected, still named "today"). The grid is one tab stop with a roving tabindex: arrows move a day, Up and Down a week, Home and End the week, Page Up and Page Down a month (Shift a year), Enter and Space select. A key that leaves the month changes the month shown. Days outside the month are empty cells. Each cell has its full accessible name (balance with the currency code, counts, "below zero", "unplaced surplus"). The data is synthetic and carried forward from an opening balance: real transactions and dated planned items only.

Calendar: actions and the change dialog

Planned, Partial and Overdue occurrences have "Mark as paid", "Link a transaction" and "Change" (all secondary: no primary button here); a Confirmed one has none; a Cancelled one has only "Restore". Each name starts with its visible words and adds the occurrence. "Change" opens the native dialog "Change this occurrence" (try the buttons: Cancel is focused first, Escape closes, the focus returns to the button; the dialog opened this way is under the section). Its choice is a three-segment control (Move, Change, Skip), the date is the date picker field, the amount the exact-syntax field, and a link button "Restore" returns an occurrence that has an override to the rule of the planned item. Skip hides the fields. The dialog is drawn in place in each frame with live controls; the focus ring on Cancel is drawn for the reference.

          English and French

          Amounts and dates come from Intl, as formatAmount and formatCalendarDate do in the app: the values below are computed by this page from integer minor units, not typed. French groups thousands with a narrow no-break space (U+202F) and puts the euro after a no-break space. The upstream Host Grotesk file has no U+202F glyph, so the shipped file maps it to the face's own thin space (0.15 em): the width no longer depends on the device, and the Intl output stays as it is. Labels are written in sentence case and uppercased by CSS, so accented capitals stay correct.
